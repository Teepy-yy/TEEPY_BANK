A banking system for my bank, TEEPY BANK. It is a banking system where users can check their bank balances, make deposits, withdraw money and send money. It displays a menu where you get four options in numbers from 1 to 4. You enter 1 to check your balance and your balance will be displayed. You enter 2 to make a deposit, you will be asked the amount of money you are depositing and then it adds the deposited money to your balance and displays you new balance. You enter 3 to make a withdrawal, it will ask you the amount and after you enter your amount it subtracts it from your balance and displays your new balance. You enter 4 to send money, it asks you to enter the account number you are sending the money to and the amount, subtracts the amount from your balance and gives you your new balance.
